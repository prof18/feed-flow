---
title: "How do I add a feed?"
weight: 30
---

Tap **Add feed** and paste one of these:

- the feed address (for example `https://example.com/feed`)
- the website address: FeedFlow finds the feed when the site advertises one
- a YouTube channel link: FeedFlow converts it to the channel's feed, and **Hide Shorts** leaves out Shorts and live streams

You can also browse **Feed Suggestions** for ready-made feeds, or import your subscriptions from another reader with an OPML file.

Some content needs a workaround:

- **Newsletters**: many providers, such as Substack and Buttondown, publish a feed for every newsletter. For the others, a service like Kill the Newsletter turns emails into a feed.
- **Websites without a feed**: tools like RSS-Bridge, RSSHub or rss.app can generate one.
