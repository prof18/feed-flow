---
title: "Can I read articles offline?"
weight: 150
---

Yes, in **Settings → Reading behavior**:

- **Prefetch article content for offline reading** downloads the full articles while feeds refresh. Refreshing takes longer and uses more data.
- **Save article content on open for offline reading** keeps every article you open.

Images are cached while you browse. You can clear downloaded articles and cached images in **Settings → Sync & storage**.
