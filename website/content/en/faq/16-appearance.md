---
title: "How do I change the theme, text size or layout?"
weight: 160
---

**Settings → Appearance**: theme (System, Light, Dark or Black), hiding unread counts, and reducing motion and animations (useful on e-ink screens).

**Settings → Feed list** has five sections:

- **Layout**: **Feed layout** (List, Card or Big image card), a grid on wide screens, and **Text scale**
- **Show in list**: turn **Description**, **Image**, **Date**, **Unread indicator** and **Feed name** on or off, choose **Description lines** (3 lines, 5 lines or no limit), and hide a title repeated in the description. The description options are available while **Description** is on.
- **Date and time**: **Date format** and **Time format**, available while **Date** is on
- **Order**: **Sort by date**, newest or oldest first
- **Gestures**: **Swipe left** and **Swipe right**

**In the reader**, the text settings button lets you change the font, font size, title size and line height.

To jump back to the top of a long list, use the scroll-to-top button. On desktop, clicking the top bar also works.
