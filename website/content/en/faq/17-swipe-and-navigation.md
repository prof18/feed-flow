---
title: "Can I swipe to mark articles as read or move between articles?"
weight: 170
---

Yes. Swipe actions are off until you choose them: in **Settings → Feed list**, pick an action for **Swipe left** and **Swipe right**: **Mark as read or unread**, **Add or remove bookmark**, or **Open in preferred browser**. Choose **None** to turn a direction off again.

Swipe actions work in the list layout. When the feed list shows articles in a grid, for example on a tablet or in landscape, swiping is off; long-press an article to mark it as read or unread or to bookmark it.

On mobile, swiping right from the edge opens the sidebar, unless you've set a swipe action for that direction.

In the reader, use the previous and next buttons (or swipe) to move between articles without going back to the list.
