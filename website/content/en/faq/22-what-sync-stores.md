---
title: "What does sync save? Are my settings synced?"
weight: 220
---

With iCloud, Dropbox or Google Drive, FeedFlow saves a file in an app-specific folder of your storage (it can't see your other files) containing your subscriptions, categories, and read and bookmarked articles. Article content isn't included.

Settings, blocked words and widget configuration stay on each device and aren't synced. The sync file isn't encrypted by FeedFlow, so it's protected by your storage account.

With a reader service (FreshRSS, Miniflux, BazQux, Feedbin), what's synced is decided by the service.
