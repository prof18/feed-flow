---

- **Android**: [Google
  Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.prof18.feedflow) un
  [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.prof18.feedflow/)
- **iPhone un iPad**: App Store, iOS 17 un iPadOS 17 vai jaunākās versijās
- **macOS**: App Store, Mac datori ar Apple procesoru
- **Windows**: [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9n5t1rfbb6v5)
  or the installer from [GitHub
  releases](https://github.com/prof18/feed-flow/releases)
- **Linux**: [Flathub](https://flathub.org/apps/com.prof18.feedflow), plus
  `.deb`, `.rpm` and AppImage packages from [GitHub
  releases](https://github.com/prof18/feed-flow/releases)

A few things aren't available:

- **Web version**: browsers can't download most feeds directly, and FeedFlow has
  no server to do it for them.
- **Intel Macs**: the frameworks FeedFlow is built with are dropping support for
  Intel Macs, so only Apple silicon is supported.
- **Android APK on GitHub**: install from Google Play or F-Droid. Obtainium can
  track the F-Droid release.

The `.deb` package targets Debian 13 and distributions based on it. On older
distributions, use the Flatpak or the AppImage.

Version numbers can differ between platforms: each platform gets its fixes as
soon as they're ready, without waiting for the others.
