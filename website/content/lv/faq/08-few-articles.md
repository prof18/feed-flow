---
title: "Why does a feed show only a few articles? Where are the older ones?"
weight: 80
---

FeedFlow shows exactly what the feed contains, and many websites only include
their latest 5 to 20 articles. Older articles are no longer in the feed, so no
reader can download them.

Other readers sometimes show more because their servers have been collecting the
feed for years. FeedFlow starts collecting when you subscribe: from then on,
articles stay in the app even after they drop out of the feed, until you delete
them or **Auto-delete old articles** removes them.

Dates and times come from the feed too. If an article has no date or the wrong
one, the feed published it that way. Articles without a date appear at the
bottom of the list.
