---
title: "How do I delete old articles?"
weight: 90
---

- **Auto-delete old articles** (Settings → Sync & storage) removes articles
  older than one day, one week, two weeks or one month.
- **Delete week-old articles**, in the menu, removes articles older than a week
  from the list you're viewing: the Timeline, a category or a single feed.
