---
title: "Why doesn't my widget update?"
weight: 120
---

The widget shows the articles FeedFlow already has, so it updates when the app
refreshes feeds.

**Android**: the widget follows **Background sync** (Settings → Sync & storage).
If it's set to **Never**, the widget only changes when you open the app. In
**Settings → Widget Configuration** you can choose what each widget shows
(Timeline, Bookmarks, a category or a single feed) and change its background
colour, opacity, text colour, font size, header and refresh button.

**iPhone and iPad**: the widget updates when FeedFlow refreshes: while you use
the app, when you leave it, and when iOS lets it run in the background, which
can take hours. Widgets can't download feeds on their own, so opening FeedFlow
is the reliable way to bring the widget up to date.
