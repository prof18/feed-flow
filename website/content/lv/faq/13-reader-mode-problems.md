---
title: "An article looks broken or incomplete in reader mode. What can I do?"
weight: 130
---

Reader mode downloads the web page and removes everything but the article. Some
pages can't be cleaned up: they need JavaScript, hide content behind a paywall
or a cookie banner, or block automated tools.

What you can do:

- **Complete the website's check**: some sites show a security check (for
  example Cloudflare) instead of the article. On Android and iOS the reader then
  offers **Open website**: complete the check and the article opens in the
  reader. If a site keeps asking, the check can't be completed, or you only ever
  get a short summary, use the feed's own content or the browser for that feed,
  as described below.
- **Report it**: in the reader, open the menu and choose **Report broken
  article**. FeedFlow sends only the article link, the app version and the
  system version, which helps improve the reader.
- **Use the feed's own content**: some feeds, such as comics, Mastodon or
  Telegram channels, already include the whole post. Open the feed's settings
  and set **Open articles in** to **Reader (feed)**.
- **Open it in the browser** for that feed: set **Open articles in** to a
  browser in the feed's settings. Every article from that feed then skips reader
  mode.

When the reader can't extract an article, FeedFlow opens it in your browser
instead. Files like PDFs, and YouTube and Telegram links, always open outside
the reader.

The reader also has a button to open the page through archive.is.
