---
title: "What's the difference between reader mode and the browser options?"
weight: 140
---

**Open articles in** decides what happens when you tap an article. Set it for
every feed in **Settings → Reading behavior**, or for a single feed in its
settings.

- **Reader (website)**: downloads the original page and shows a clean, readable
  version inside FeedFlow.
- **Reader (feed)**: shows the content included in the feed, without downloading
  the page. Best for feeds that contain the full text.
- **In-app browser**: opens the page without leaving FeedFlow. On Android it's
  provided by your default browser (Chrome, Firefox and so on), so it uses that
  browser's settings, cookies and VPN rules. On iPhone and iPad it's Safari.
- **Favourite browser**: opens the page in a browser you pick.
- **Default browser**: opens the page in the system's default browser.

The Linux app has no in-app browser: you can choose reader mode or a browser.
