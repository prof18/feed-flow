---
title: "Can I read articles offline?"
weight: 150
---

Yes, in **Settings → Reading behavior**:

- **Make offline copies when feeds refresh** gets the full article from each
  website every time feeds refresh. Refreshing takes longer and uses more data.
- **Keep offline copies of articles you open** keeps every article you open.

Images are cached while you browse. You can delete offline copies and clear
cached images in **Settings → Sync & storage**.
