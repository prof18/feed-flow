---
title: "Can I read articles offline?"
weight: 150
---

Yes. On Android and iOS, the options are in **Settings → Storage → Offline
copies**. On desktop, they are in **Settings → Reading behavior**:

- **Make offline copies on refresh** gets the full article from each website
  every time feeds refresh. Refreshing takes longer and uses more data.
- **Keep articles you open offline** keeps every article you open.

Offline copies are used when an article opens in reader mode, also for feeds
whose **Feed settings** open them in reader mode.

Images are cached while you browse. You can delete offline copies and clear
cached images in **Settings → Storage**. **Delete offline copies** also removes
the copies of bookmarked articles.
