---
title: "Can I swipe to mark articles as read or move between articles?"
weight: 170
---

Yes. In **Settings → Feed list**, choose a **Left swipe** and **Right swipe**
action: toggle read, toggle bookmark, or open in your preferred browser.

On mobile, swiping right from the edge opens the sidebar, unless you've set a
swipe action for that direction.

In the reader, use the previous and next buttons (or swipe) to move between
articles without going back to the list.
