---
title: "How do bookmarks work? Is there a read-later list?"
weight: 180
---

Bookmarks are the read-later list: add an article from its menu, with a swipe
action, or from the reader. You'll find them in **Bookmarks**, in the Library
section of the sidebar. Bookmarked articles are never removed by automatic
deletion.

To move bookmarks to another device, export them from **Import and export →
Articles (CSV)** and import the file there, after adding the same feeds.

FeedFlow only saves articles from your feeds. To save any link from the web and
read it later, even offline, try [ReaderFlow](https://www.readerflow.app),
FeedFlow's companion read-it-later app for Android, iPhone, iPad and Mac.
