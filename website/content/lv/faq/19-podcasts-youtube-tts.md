---
title: "Does FeedFlow support podcasts, YouTube and reading articles aloud?"
weight: 190
---

- **YouTube**: paste a channel link to subscribe, and turn on **Hide Shorts** to
  skip Shorts and live streams.
- **Podcasts**: episodes with audio show an audio badge. On Android, iPhone and
  iPad they play in FeedFlow, with playback speed and a Now playing bar;
  elsewhere, **Open audio** opens them in another app.
- **Listen to article**: on Android, iPhone and iPad, the reader can read the
  article aloud.
