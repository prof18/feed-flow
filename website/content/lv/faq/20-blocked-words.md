---
title: "Can I hide articles about topics I'm not interested in?"
weight: 200
---

Yes. Add words to **Settings → Feeds & accounts → Blocked words**: articles with
any of these words in the title or description are hidden.

You can also hide a whole feed from the Timeline (in its settings, **Hide this
feed's articles from the timeline**), and hide YouTube Shorts per channel.

Blocked words are stored on the device and aren't synced with your other
devices.
