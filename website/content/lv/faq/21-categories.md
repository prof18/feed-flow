---
title: "Kā es varu organizēt savas ziņu plūsmas?"
weight: 210
---

- **Categories**: pick a category when you add a feed, or change it later in the
  feed's settings. Long press a category in the sidebar to rename or delete it.
  Deleting a category moves its feeds to Uncategorized; **Delete all feeds**
  removes the feeds too.
- To **edit a feed** on Android or iOS, go to **Settings → Feeds & accounts →
  Feeds** and tap the feed to open **Feed settings**. You can also find **Feed
  settings** by long pressing the feed in the sidebar (right click on desktop).
  Use it to rename the feed or change its category, feed link, link opening,
  notifications and more. Switches and the link opening option apply as soon as
  you change them; **Save** confirms a new name, feed link or category. **Hide
  Shorts** changes the feed link, so it needs **Save** too.
- **Piespraudiet** ziņu plūsmas, kuras lasāt visbiežāk, lai tās vienmēr būtu
  sānjoslas augšdaļā.
- **Reorder** categories with the Reorder button in the sidebar.
- On **desktop**, select several feeds to move or delete them together, or drag
  a feed onto a category.

Ziņu plūsma vienlaikus var būt tikai vienā kategorijā.
