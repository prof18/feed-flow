Read, sync, organize.

Read your way

Open articles in your default browser, or pick a separate one for reading. Reader mode strips the page down to the text, with adjustable size. Full articles can be fetched ahead of time for offline reading.

Sync the way you already do

Keep everything on one device, or sync through iCloud, Dropbox or Google Drive. Already run a feed server? FeedFlow connects to FreshRSS, Miniflux, Feedbin and BazQux.

Keep it tidy

Import and export OPML to move in or out of any other reader. Group feeds into categories, bookmark articles for later, hide articles containing words you block, and search everything you've fetched.
